02 — Overview
The Brand

Where pages
become pixels.

DigiSkills is a digital-learning platform that turns curiosity into capability. The identity had to bridge two worlds — the timeless authority of education and the energy of technology. The answer is a single confident symbol: an open book whose pages lift away as glowing pixels.

04 — Concept
Logo Anatomy

An idea taking flight.

1

The open book

The foundation of the mark — instantly legible as learning, knowledge and study. A wide, confident V grounds the whole identity.

2

Pixels in flight

The top pages break away into amber squares — knowledge digitised, ideas sparking, skills levelling up. The single visual move that makes it DigiSkills, not just a book.

3

Two-colour clarity

Deep navy for trust and depth; warm amber for energy and optimism. The contrast keeps the mark punchy at any size.

05 — Construction
Built on a Grid

Balanced by design.

Every line is measured. The pixel field, the book mouth, the central axis and the baseline are locked to a fixed modular grid — so the book's symmetry, the angle of the pages and the rhythm of the pixel trail rebuild identically every time.

06 — Spacing
Clearspace & Minimum Size

Protected space.

Keep a minimum exclusion zone of 1× the width of one pixel-square on every side. Nothing should intrude into this margin.

12 — Misuse
Logo Misuse

Protect the mark.

The logo's strength comes from consistency. Avoid these six common mistakes — when in doubt, reach for an approved master file rather than recreating the mark.

×
Don't stretch, squash or distort the proportions.
×
Don't rotate or tilt the mark off its baseline.
×
Don't recolour outside the brand palette.
×
Don't add drop shadows, glows or other effects.
×
Don't place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
×
Don't flatten the two-tone mark into one muddy colour.
