Every carousel fights the same battle: the first panel has to earn the swipe. I built this one around Russell Brunson's Hook, Story, Offer, one idea per swipe.

### Hook
Panel one is a line, not a car: *Street Legal. But Just Barely.* It's a small contradiction, and contradictions stop thumbs. The car stays hidden on purpose. The only way to see it is to swipe.

### Story
The four panels are one continuous artboard, sliced at 1080 × 1350. The smoke and the horizon carry across every cut, so each swipe feels like the same scene moving, not a new slide loading. The specs arrive one per panel, in the order that builds the case: the Nürburgring lap time, then top speed, then 815 horsepower.

### Offer
A concept piece has no price tag, so the offer is the brand itself. The last panel drops the car and leaves only the running-horse mark in the smoke. That's the thing I want people to remember. *Self-initiated concept, not affiliated with Ford.*
