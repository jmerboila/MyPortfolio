A spec poster can easily become a spreadsheet with a car on it. I used Hook, Story, Offer to decide what gets seen first, second and last.

### Hook
The oversized LEXUS wordmark runs straight across a deep red vertical band. It's big type crossing a hard edge on a pale ground, and that's what the eye lands on first.

### Story
The white NX 350h sits in profile across both halves of the layout, with ghosted front and rear views behind it. Then the numbers do the persuading: **240 HP** and **200 km/h**, set bigger than anything but the name, and a short paragraph on the hybrid powertrain and the red leather cabin.

### Offer
The poster ends where the conversation should continue: Facebook, X, Instagram and YouTube along the bottom. Once you've read this far, the next step is to follow.
