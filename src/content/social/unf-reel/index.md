Eight seconds to say *one term is done and the next is ready*. That's a message, not a montage, so I cut it as Hook, Story, Offer.

### Hook
It opens on a highway at night, with headlights and motion from the first frame. It's dark and familiar, the end of something.

### Story
The drive turns into daylight: open road, fields, traffic cones, a yellow school bus, a street arch over a small downtown. The UNF wave logo rides along on the road the whole way, so the brand is part of the journey, not stuck on at the end. Night to day is the term changing.

### Offer
The drive arrives at the illuminated UNF sign on the corner of a glass building. That's the destination, and the caption finishes the thought: *Term 2: done. Term 3: ready.*
