Same poster, new job. In the feed a static image competes with video, so this version builds itself in front of you, and the build order *is* the Hook, Story, Offer.

### Hook
It starts moving immediately: the red band rises through the frame. There's no waiting on a static first frame, so there's no reason to scroll past it.

### Story
Everything arrives in order of importance. First the emblem, then the LEXUS wordmark sliding in above *NX 350h*, then the car gliding into profile, then the specs (240 HP, 200 km/h) and the description. Ten seconds, silent by design, so it works with the sound off.

### Offer
It settles on the finished poster, social icons and all, and holds. Anyone who has seen the static version recognises it, so the two posts reinforce each other.
