Ten seconds, no sound, and it still has to work in a feed where most people scroll with the sound off. Hook, Story, Offer kept it tight.

### Hook
Motion from the first frame. A pale silhouette pushes out of white haze. No title card or logo, just enough shape to make you wait for the rest.

### Story
The car sharpens into a dark, low-slung shape under the rear wing. Then the whole frame flips to black and the car dissolves back into smoke. The story is the reveal and the exit, the same *just barely* tension as the carousel, told in motion.

### Offer
It resolves on the running-horse outline alone, above a white floor. The last thing on screen is the brand, and because the video loops, it's also the moment that leads straight back into the hook.
