problem: "Two letters, J and M, had to read as one mark, with the J leading and the M working with it rather than against it."
approach: "I tried four directions first. JM set plainly in Bask Old Face was close, but something was missing. A fused ligature lost the J. A script Jme felt separated. A heavy JME was too bold. So I went back to JM and let the J lead: Perpetua Titling MT Light, the J made taller and dropped below the baseline, the M made smaller. Then I offset every letter by about 5 pt and used Shape Builder to cut the J where the M's thick diagonal crosses it, so the J passes behind the diagonal and in front of the thin stem."
outcome: "The monogram is my mark across this site: the favicon, the header, and the avatar on every social post shown here. The build became the Reel on this page."
constraints:
  - "Two letters, one mark: neither one gets lost"
  - "The J leads; the M supports it"
  - "Clean enough to work small, as an avatar and a favicon"
