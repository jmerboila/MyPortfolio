05 — The System
Dual Colorway System

Two versions. Total flexibility.

The identity ships as a matched pair so it stays crisp on any surface: solid black for light backgrounds, pure white for dark ones. Always choose the version with the strongest contrast.

Black · for light backgrounds
White · for dark backgrounds
06 — Construction
Built on a Baseline

Engineered alignment.

Every glyph — including the 8 — is locked to a shared set of guides: a common cap height, x-height, baseline and descender line, with matched side bearings. The result reproduces identically from favicon to façade.

07 — Spacing
Clearspace & Minimum Size

Give the name air.

Maintain an exclusion zone equal to the cap height of "D" on every side. Keep the wordmark clear of competing type, edges and busy imagery.

Digital · 120px min width

11 — Usage
Do's & Don'ts

Keep it sharp.

✓ Do
  • ✓ Use the black wordmark on light backgrounds.
  • ✓ Use the white wordmark on dark or photographic fields.
  • ✓ Always pick the version with the highest contrast.
  • ✓ Respect the cap-height clearspace on all sides.
✕ Don't
  • ✕ Place the black mark on dark / low-contrast grounds.
  • ✕ Condense, stretch or skew the wordmark.
  • ✕ Recolour the letters outside black or white.
  • ✕ Add shadows, outlines or gradients.
