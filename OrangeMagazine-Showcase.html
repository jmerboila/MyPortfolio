02 — Overview
The Brand

Fresh stories,
millions
watching.

Orange Magazine is a digital-media platform built to deliver fresh, relevant and truthful content that resonates with a diverse audience — Gen-Z and teens included. It spotlights local and international personalities, from K-Pop groups to global acts like Westlife and Bruno Mars, and has earned millions of views across social platforms. The identity had to match that energy: confident as a profile photo, instantly recognisable as a thumbnail, and warm enough to feel like a friend in the feed.

03 — The Mark
Primary Logo

The full-colour, horizontal lockup is the hero of the system — the version that should be used wherever space and reproduction allow.

04 — Concept
Logo Anatomy

The "O" does the talking.

1

Letter-as-object substitution

The leading "O" of ORANGE is replaced by a ripe orange with two fresh leaves — a visual pun that locks the name into memory the instant it's read.

2

Stacked editorial masthead

ORANGE sits above MAGAZINE in a tightly-tracked, heavyweight grotesque. The two-tier lockup reads like a cover masthead, reinforcing the publishing category.

3

Two inks, total clarity

Orange carries the personality; black carries the authority. Nothing competes — the icon is the only spot of colour, so the eye goes straight to it.

05 — Construction
Built on a Grid

Geometry you can trust.

Every line is measured. The leaf tip, cap height, the baseline of ORANGE and the baseline of MAGAZINE are locked to a fixed modular grid — keeping proportions, the leaf angle and optical weight consistent every time the masthead is rebuilt or scaled.

06 — Spacing
Clearspace & Minimum Size

Give it room to breathe.

Maintain a minimum exclusion zone of 1× — equal to the height of the icon — on all sides. No type, image or competing graphic should enter this zone.

11 — Usage
Do's & Don'ts

Protect the mark.

✓ Do
  • ✓ Use the primary lockup on clean, uncluttered backgrounds.
  • ✓ Switch to the reversed version on dark or photographic fields.
  • ✓ Respect the 1× clearspace on every side.
  • ✓ Keep the official Orange Pulp (F26B1F) consistent across media.
✕ Don't
  • ✕ Recolour the icon or swap in unapproved hues.
  • ✕ Stretch, squash or rotate the lockup.
  • ✕ Add drop shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • ✕ Place the full-colour mark on busy, low-contrast imagery.
